# Credits

## Salamander Grand Piano

Piano sounds are played from recordings of the **Salamander Grand Piano V3+**
(Yamaha C5) by **Alexander Holm**, licensed under
[CC-BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

- Source: https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html
- The recordings were transcoded (WAV → mono AAC), trimmed, and faded for the
  app's sound packs; see `scripts/build-sample-packs.sh`.

## ZIPFoundation

Sound pack archives are extracted with
[ZIPFoundation](https://github.com/weichsel/ZIPFoundation) by Thomas Zoechling,
MIT license.
